The Hidden Hassle Behind Every Home Puja

Urban families want to perform regular pujas — but the process is so chaotic and time-consuming that most give up.

A Typical Urban Family's Struggle

  1. Frantically search for a reliable pandit available on the exact date & time.
  2. Get a long samagri list from the pandit.
  3. Visit 3–4 different shops: dry samagri, fresh flowers/fruits, prasad ingredients, decor items.
  4. Worry about quality, purity, overpricing, and missing items.
  5. Spend hours coordinating everything — leaving no energy to actually enjoy the puja.

Result: Families perform far fewer pujas than they truly desire.
